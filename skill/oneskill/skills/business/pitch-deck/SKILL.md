# Pitch Deck

## What investors are deciding
Whether this could be very large, whether this team can do it, and why now. Every slide answers one of those. Ten to twelve slides; they will give it about three minutes before a meeting.

## Order
1. **Title** - company, one line on what you do in plain words.
2. **Problem** - who has it, how painful, how they cope today.
3. **Solution** - the product, shown. A screenshot beats a diagram.
4. **Why now** - what changed in technology, regulation or behaviour.
5. **Market** - built bottom-up: customers x price. Skip the "1% of a trillion" slide.
6. **Traction** - the strongest real number you have, as a chart over time. Label the axis honestly.
7. **Business model** - who pays, how much, unit economics if known.
8. **Competition** - how customers choose, and why you win a specific segment. Not a grid where you have every tick.
9. **Go-to-market** - how the next 100 customers arrive.
10. **Team** - why you, specifically, for this problem.
11. **Ask** - how much, what it buys, the milestones it reaches.

## Rules
- Each slide title is the claim, readable alone.
- Real numbers only. Label projections as projections. Never invent traction.
- Lead with your strongest slide early; if traction is exceptional, move it to slide two.
- Prepare an appendix for financials, cohort data and detailed market sizing.
