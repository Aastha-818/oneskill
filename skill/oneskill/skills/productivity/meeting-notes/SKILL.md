# Meeting Notes

## What readers want
People who were absent want to know what was decided and what they owe. Write for them. Nobody wants a replay of the conversation.

## Format
- **Header** - meeting name, date, who attended.
- **Summary** - two or three sentences: the purpose and the outcome.
- **Decisions** - each as a full sentence, with the reasoning in a clause if it was contested.
- **Action items** - a table or list: task, owner, due date. One owner per item. If no owner or date was agreed, write "unassigned" or "no date" - do not guess.
- **Open questions** - what was raised and not settled, and who will resolve it.
- **Notes** - by topic, briefly, only what someone might need later.

## Rules
- Record what was said, not what you think should have been decided.
- Attribute decisions and commitments to people; leave out who said what in ordinary discussion.
- Keep numbers, dates and names exact. Mark anything unclear in the source as "[unclear]" rather than smoothing it over.
- Drop small talk, repetition and tangents.
- Neutral tone - no judging contributions.

## Follow-up
If asked, draft a short email: the summary, the decisions and each person's actions. Keep it under 150 words.
