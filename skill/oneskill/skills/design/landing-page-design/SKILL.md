# Landing Page Design

## Before any pixels
Write one sentence each for: who lands here, what they already believe, and the single action you want. If you cannot name the action, the page has no job yet - ask.

## Structure
1. **Hero** - a headline that states the outcome, not the category ("Ship invoices in 30 seconds", not "Modern invoicing platform"). One supporting line. One primary button. Show the product, not an abstract illustration.
2. **Proof** - logos, a number, or one quote, directly under the hero.
3. **How it works** - three steps at most, each with a real screenshot or artifact.
4. **Objections** - answer the top three reasons someone would not act (price, effort, trust).
5. **Repeat the action** - same button, same words as the hero.

## Visual direction
- Pick a point of view before colours: editorial, technical, playful, luxurious. Everything follows from it.
- One typeface family with real contrast in size (hero at least 3x body). One accent colour used only for actions.
- Spacing does the work of boxes and dividers. Remove a border before adding one.
- Avoid the defaults that read as template: purple-to-blue gradients, three identical icon cards, centred everything, stock 3D blobs.

## Build
- Mobile first; the hero must fit a phone screen with the button visible.
- Real copy from the start, never lorem ipsum - layout decisions depend on word length.
- Check contrast (4.5:1 for body), focus states, and that the page works with images off.

## Finish check
Read only the headlines top to bottom. If they do not tell the whole story alone, rewrite them.
