# Copy Editing

## Order of work
Fix big things before small ones - there is no point polishing a sentence you will cut.
1. **Purpose** - can you state what the piece is for and who reads it? If not, ask.
2. **Structure** - is the main point in the first paragraph? Does each paragraph do one job? Reorder before rewording.
3. **Sentences** - cut filler ("in order to", "the fact that"), turn nouns back into verbs, split anything over about 30 words, replace vague words with the specific thing.
4. **Mechanics** - grammar, spelling, punctuation, consistency of names, numbers, capitalisation and tense.

## Keep the author
The goal is their writing, better - not yours. Keep their vocabulary, rhythm and opinions. Do not add claims, facts or examples they did not make. If something is unclear, flag it instead of guessing what they meant.

## How to return it
- Give the edited text first, clean and ready to use.
- Then a short list of the changes that matter and why - not every comma.
- List separately anything you were unsure about, or facts worth checking.
- If asked for a light proofread, change only errors.

Match the variety of English already in the draft (British, American, Indian).
