# Linkedin Post

## What works
One idea, from one person's real experience, in plain words. The feed rewards the first two lines - everything after "see more" is earned by them.

## Shape
- **Hook (lines 1-2)** - a specific claim, number or moment. "I lost a 4 lakh client over one email." Not "Excited to share some thoughts on communication".
- **Story or evidence** - what happened, in short lines. Concrete details: who, when, how much.
- **Turn** - what you understood that you did not before.
- **Takeaway** - one usable line the reader can apply today.
- **Close** - a real question you want answered, or nothing. Skip "Agree?".

## Style
- Short paragraphs, one to two lines, white space between.
- Write how the author talks. No "thrilled", "humbled", "game-changer".
- At most three hashtags, at the end. No emoji bullets down the left margin.
- 120 to 220 words. Longer only when the story needs it.

## Do not invent
Never make up a client, number or anecdote. If the user has not given a real experience, ask for one, or write an opinion or how-to post that does not need one.

Offer two hook options for the same post so the user can choose.
